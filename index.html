About Scoopy

Small shop. Big scoops. Since 2018.

Our Story

Scoopy started in 2018 with one small cart and one big dream: make ice cream that tastes like childhood. Our first customers were the kids from the school next door, and many of them still visit us every weekend.

Today we still make every batch by hand in small quantities, using fresh milk, real fruit and no artificial colours. If a flavour is not good enough to serve our own family, it does not go on the menu.

  • 25+ Flavours
  • 100% Fresh Milk
  • 5000+ Happy Customers
  • 8 Years Of Sweetness
